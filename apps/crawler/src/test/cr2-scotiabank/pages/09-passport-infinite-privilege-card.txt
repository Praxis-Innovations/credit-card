About the offer

To qualify for this Offer make sure to open a new Scotiabank Passport® Visa Infinite Privilege* credit card account (“Account”) between May 1, 2026 and November 1, 2026.

To qualify for up to 80,000 bonus Scene+ points:

- Earn 30,000 bonus Scene+ points by making at least $3,000 in everyday eligible purchases in your first 3 months.
- Earn an additional 30,000 bonus Scene+ points when you spend at least $20,000 in everyday eligible purchases in your first 6 months.
- Earn an additional 20,000 bonus Scene+ points when you make at least one eligible purchase that is posted to your new Account during the 14th month after the month the Account was opened.

Once you qualify, we’ll credit the applicable Scene+ membership account in up to 3 instalments:

- 30,000 bonus Scene+ points will be credited to the applicable Scene+ membership account within 8 weeks after the qualifying $3,000 in eligible purchases being posted to your Account within the first 3 months of Account opening.
- If you qualify, an additional 30,000 bonus Scene+ points will be credited to the applicable Scene+ membership account within approximately 8 weeks after you have met the $20,000 spend threshold in your Account within the first 6 months of Account opening.
- If you qualify, an additional 20,000 bonus Scene+ points will be credited to the applicable Scene+ membership account within approximately 8 weeks after the first eligible purchase has been posted to your new Account during the 14th month after the month the Account was opened.

Note: For joint Accounts, the applicable bonus will be credited to the Scene+ account linked to the Account of the borrower that completed the last transaction to meet the qualifying amount of eligible purchases in the case of the 30,000 Point Bonus and the Additional 30,000 Point Bonus. In the case of the Additional 20,000 Point Bonus, the bonus will be credited to the Scene+ account linked to the Account of the borrower that completed the first transaction that qualifies as an eligible purchase posted to the Account during the 14th month after the month the Account was opened. However, if the qualifying purchase for any bonus is completed by a supplementary cardholder, in all cases the applicable bonus will be credited to the Primary Borrower’s Scene+ account linked to the Account.

Individuals who are currently or were previously primary or secondary cardholders of a Scotiabank personal credit card in the past 2 years, including those that switch from an existing Scotiabank personal credit card, as well as employees of Scotiabank, are not eligible for this offer.

How do I use my credit card to enter an airport lounge?

To enter an airport lounge with a Scotiabank Passport Visa Infinite + or Scotiabank Passport Visa Infinite Privilege card, you first need to sign up for the Visa Airport Companion Program (powered by DragonPass). Once you sign up, a Membership ID linked to your card will be created under the DragonPass Membership Profile. To find out more about the Visa Airport Companion Program and other benefits offered with these cards, visit us online at Scotiabank Passport Visa Infinite + Card or Scotiabank Passport Visa Infinite Privilege Card.

To enter lounges accessible through Priority Pass with your American Express Platinum card, show your Priority Pass membership card (physical or digital) and present your credit card to access the Plaza Premium lounges. To find out more about the Priority Pass Access Lounge Program and other benefits offered with the Scotiabank American Express Platinum Card, visit us online at Scotiabank Platinum American Express® Card.

How do I sign up for airport lounge benefits on my credit card?

To sign up for the Visa Airport Companion Program (powered by DragonPass), download the Visa Airport Companion App or visit the program’s website (visaairportcompanion.caopens in a new tab).

To sign up for airport lounge benefits for your Scotiabank American Express Platinum card, visit prioritypass.com/scotiabankplatinum and provide the code “Scotia1” for primary cardholders and “Scotia2” for supplementary cardholders.

How can I learn more about using Scene+ for travel?

Book travel using Scene+ points with Scene+ travel powered by Expedia. You can also apply your Scene+ points to offset other travel costs using the Apply Points to Travel option — up to one year after purchase.

To change a booking, you made using Scene+ points, please contact Scene+ Travel powered by Expedia at 1-866-586-2805 for help with your request.

For more information on travel with Scene+, visit the Scene+ websiteopens in a new tab.

- Hotels: 3 Scene+ Points per $1 spent;
- Car Rental: 3 Scene+ Points per $1 spent
- Things to Do: 3 Scene+ Points per $1 spent

Dining and Entertainment Purchases: You are awarded two (2) Scene+ Points for every eligible $1.00 purchase on all dining and entertainment purchases charged and posted to the Account (the earn rates for each of the categories are referred to as the “Accelerated Earn Rates”).

Regular Earn Rate: You are awarded one (1) Scene+ Point for every $1.00 in all other purchases of goods and services charged to the Account (the “Regular Earn Rate”).

Merchant Classifications: You will earn the Accelerated Earn Rates on purchases made on your account at merchants classified in the Visa credit card network with a Merchant Category Code (“MCC”) in the “Travel” category (the “travel purchases”) (including Airlines and Air Carriers, Car Rental Agencies, Lodging – Hotels, Motels, Resorts, Local and Suburban Commuter Passenger Transportation, including Ferries, Passenger Railways, Taxicabs and Limousines, Bus Lines, Steamship and Cruise Lines, Airlines and Air Carriers – Not Elsewhere Classified, Travel Agencies and Tour Operators, Tolls and Bridge Fees, Transportation Services – Not Elsewhere Classified, Lodging – Hotels, Motels, Resorts, Central Reservation Services not elsewhere classified, Timeshares, Trailer parks and Campgrounds, Automobile Rental Agency and Motel Home and Recreational Vehicle Rentals ); or in the “Dining” category (“Dining Purchases”) (including Eating Places and Restaurants, Drinking Places and Fast Food Restaurants); or in the “Entertainment” category (the “Entertainment Purchases”) (including Motion Picture Theatres, Ticket Agencies and Theatrical Producers, Ticket Agencies, Bands, Orchestras, Miscellaneous Entertainers - Not Elsewhere Classified, Tourist Attractions and Exhibits, Aquariums, Seaquariums, Dolphinariums, and Zoos categories). MCC are set by the Visa credit card network associated with your Account and must be recognized by Scotiabank’s system as set out in the Scene+ Program terms and conditions. MCC are subject to change.
