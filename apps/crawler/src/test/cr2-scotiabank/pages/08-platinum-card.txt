About the offer

To qualify for these offers make sure to open a new Scotiabank Platinum American Express credit card account (“Account”) between July 2, 2026 and November 1, 2026.

To qualify for the 100,000 bonus Scene+ points:

- Earn 60,000 bonus Scene+ points by making at least $5,000 in everyday eligible purchases in your first 3 months.
- You are eligible to earn a 20,000 Scene+ point bonus when you spend at least $10,000 in everyday eligible purchases in your first 6 months.
- Earn an additional 20,000 bonus Scene+ points when you make at least one eligible purchase that is posted to your new Account during the 14th month after the month the Account was opened.

Once you qualify, we’ll credit your account in 3 instalments:

- 60,000 bonus Scene+ points will be credited to the applicable Scene+ membership account approximately 8 weeks after the qualifying $5,000 in eligible purchases being posted to your Account within the first 3 months of Account opening.
- An additional 20,000 bonus Scene+ points will be credited to the applicable Scene+ membership account approximately 8 weeks after you have met the $10,000 spend threshold in your Account within the first 6 months of Account opening.
- If you qualify, an additional 20,000 bonus Scene+ points will be credited to the applicable Scene+ membership account within approximately 8 weeks after the first eligible purchase has been posted to your new Account during the 14th month after the month the Account was opened.

Note: For joint Accounts, the applicable bonus will be credited to the Scene+ account linked to the Account of the borrower that completed the last transaction to meet the qualifying amount of eligible purchases in the case of the 60,000 Point Bonus and the 20,000 Point Bonus. In the case of the Additional 20,000 Point Bonus, the bonus will be credited to the Scene+ account linked to the Account of the borrower that completed the first transaction that qualifies as an eligible purchase posted to the Account during the 14th month after the month the Account was opened. However, if the qualifying purchase for any bonus is completed by a supplementary cardholder, in all cases the applicable bonus will be credited to the Primary Borrower’s Scene+ account linked to the Account.

Individuals who are currently or were previously primary or secondary cardholders of a Scotiabank personal credit card in the past 2 years, including those that switch from an existing Scotiabank personal credit card, as well as employees of Scotiabank, are not eligible for this offer.

How do I use my credit card to enter an airport lounge?

To enter lounges accessible through Priority Pass with your American Express Platinum card, show your Priority Pass membership card (physical or digital) and present your credit card to access the Plaza Premium lounges. To find out more about the Priority Pass Access Lounge Program and other benefits offered with the Scotiabank American Express Platinum Card, visit us online Scotiabank Platinum American Express® Card.

How do I sign up for airport lounge benefits on my credit card?

To sign up for airport lounge benefits for your Scotiabank American Express Platinum card, visit prioritypass.com/scotiabankplatinumopens in a new tab and provide the code “Scotia1” for primary cardholders and “Scotia2” for supplementary cardholders.

How can I learn more about using Scene+ for travel?

Book travel using Scene+ points with Scene+ travel powered by Expedia. You can also apply your Scene+ points to offset other travel costs using the Apply Points to Travel option — up to one year after purchase.

To change a booking, you made using Scene+ points, please contact Scene+ Travel powered by Expedia at 1-866-586-2805 for help with your request.
