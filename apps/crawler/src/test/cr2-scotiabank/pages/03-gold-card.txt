About the offer

To qualify for these offers make sure to open a new Scotiabank Gold American Express credit card account (“Account”) between July 2, 2026 and November 1, 2026.

To qualify for the 50,000 bonus Scene+ points offer:

- Earn 30,000 bonus Scene+ points by making at least $2,000 in everyday eligible purchases in your first 3 months.
- You are eligible to earn an additional 20,000 Scene+ point bonus when you spend at least $7,500 in everyday eligible purchases in your first year.

Once you qualify, we’ll credit your account in 2 instalments:

- 30,000 bonus Scene+ points will be credited to the applicable Scene+ membership account approximately 8 weeks after the qualifying $2,000 in eligible purchases being posted to your Account within the first 3 months of Account opening.
- An additional 20,000 bonus Scene+ points will be credited to the applicable Scene+ membership account approximately 8 weeks after you have met the $7,500 annual spend threshold in your Account in your first year.

Note: For joint Accounts, the applicable bonus will be credited to the Scene+ account linked to the Account of the borrower that completed the last transaction to meet the qualifying amount of eligible purchases in the case of the 30,000 Point Bonus and the Additional 20,000 Point Bonus.

Individuals who are currently or were previously primary or secondary cardholders of a Scotiabank personal credit card in the past 2 years, including those that switch from an existing Scotiabank personal credit card, as well as employees of Scotiabank, are not eligible for this offer.

See terms and conditions below for full details.

Which credit cards give me access to airport lounges?

The Scotiabank Passport Visa Infinite +, Scotiabank Passport Visa Infinite Privilege, and Scotiabank American Express Platinum cards give you access to airport lounges.

The Scotiabank Passport Visa Infinite + card gives primary cardholders a complimentary membership in the Visa Airport Companion Program, plus six complimentary lounge visits per year from the date of enrollment. The Scotiabank Passport Visa Infinite Privilege card gives the primary and each additional cardholder a complimentary membership in the Visa Airport Companion Program, plus ten complimentary lounge visits per year from the date of enrollment. This program is powered by DragonPass, and provides access to 1,200+ airport lounges globally, including participating Plaza Premium Lounges. These lounge visits are eligible for the primary cardholder and a travel companion.

Scotiabank American Express Platinum cardholders can visit lounges in more than 100 countries and 300 cities worldwide. Primary cardholders get a complimentary membership to Priority Pass, plus 10 free visits per year from the date of enrollment. Supplementary cardholders get a Priority Pass membership and 4 free visits per year.

How do I activate my card?

To activate a Scotiabank Visa or American Express credit card, visit our online activation page.

To activate a Mastercard, please call 1-866-583-6289.

How do I add a supplementary card?

- From your Accounts page, select your credit card account
