Purchase Security & Extended Warranty Protection

Most new purchases made with your card are protected against theft, loss, or damage for 90 days from the purchase date.

Plus, when you charge the full cost of most items to your card, extended warranty coverage can double the original manufacturer’s warranty for up to one year.

Items covered by a manufacturer’s warranty repair services of five years or more must be registered within the first year of purchase.

Coverage is limited to the lesser of the repair or replacement cost not exceeding the purchase price charged to the Account and is in excess of any other insurance coverage.

What is a foreign currency conversion fee?

When you make a purchase or cash advance in a foreign currency other than your credit card’s, you may be charged a foreign currency conversion fee in addition to the exchange rate that applies. Foreign currency transactions can occur online or outside of Canada, depending upon your card's currency.

Note: If you have a Scotiabank U.S. Dollar credit card account, transactions are processed in U.S. Dollars on your account.

Please review the Fees at a glance page on our website for current information about the foreign currency conversion fee.

To see if foreign currency conversion fees apply to your card, you can review your Credit Agreement and the disclosure statement for your credit card account. You may also want to check if one of our Scotiabank credit cards with no foreign currency conversion works for you.

How do I transfer different currencies between two bank accounts?

Select either U.S. dollars or Canadian dollars after you enter the amount you would like to transfer. You can select the calculate button to convert both currencies before you complete your transfer.

What are your foreign exchange rates?

Visit the Foreign Currency Exchange Rates page on our website to see the daily foreign exchange rates for over 25 currencies.

How do I activate my credit card?

To activate a Visa or American Express card:

- Activate online: follow the steps on our online activation page
- Make an in-store purchase: insert your card and enter your PIN (check the sticker on your card for eligibility)

For Mastercard:

- Activate on the phone: call 1-866-583-6289

Once activated, you can use your card right away.

How do I add a supplementary credit card?

To add a supplementary card to your credit card account:

- From your Accounts page, select your credit card account
- Select Additional Services
- Select Add Supplementary Cards and follow the instructions
